ALPHA 1.29.1Fixed weapon-switch/reload edge cases: unfinished reloads no longer silently consume allocated Hi-Power reserve rounds when interrupted by a swap; an empty equipped weapon with reserve ammunition automatically starts reloading; weapon cooldown resets on switching. Corrected magazine persistence to respect each weapon’s upgraded capacity and always save reload completion for both pistols. Armoury magazine capacity now reflects purchased magazine upgrades.
ALPHA 1.29.0Implemented persistent, purchasable weapon upgrades for the Colt M1911A1 and Browning Hi-Power prototype: 10 upgrade categories with five tiers each. M1911A1 tier prices are 10, 15, 20, 25 and 30 credits; Hi-Power tier prices are 15, 21, 27, 33 and 39 credits. Purchases are saved per weapon and persist between missions. Upgrades affect magazine capacity, fire rate, damage, reload speed, critical chance and damage, accuracy, range, penetration and stagger. Existing map/collision, loadout, ammo persistence, aim assist and developer ammo override retained.
